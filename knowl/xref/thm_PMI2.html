Theorem 8.7. Generalized PMI.

Let \(P(a), P(a+1), P(a+2), \ldots\) be a sequence of statements, one for each integer greater than or equal to \(a\text{.}\) Assume that
  1. \(P(a)\) is true, and
    🔗
    🔗
  2. for all \(k\geq a\text{,}\) if \(P(k)\) is true, then \(P(k+1)\) is true.

    🔗
🔗
Then \(P(n)\) is true for all integers \(n \geq a\text{.}\)
